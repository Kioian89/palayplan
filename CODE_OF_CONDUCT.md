# PalayPlan Community Standards & Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **PalayPlan** community pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free, empowering, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, spoken language (including English, Filipino/Tagalog, and Bisaya/Cebuano), or farming background.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community dedicated to supporting Filipino smallholder rice farmers, researchers, agricultural technicians, and developers.

---

## Community Standards

### 🌾 The Farmer-First Principle
PalayPlan exists to empower real rice farmers in the field. All contributions, agronomic recommendations, UI designs, and feature discussions must respect:
- **Clarity & Accessibility**: Plain language over complex jargon; low bandwidth, offline-first reliability over heavy assets.
- **Agronomic Accuracy**: Adherence to validated agricultural science (PhilRice PalayCheck, IRRI, and Department of Agriculture guidelines).
- **Trilingual Inclusivity**: Ensuring features and error messages are accessible in English, Filipino, and Bisaya.

### Positive Behaviors
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other contributors, field extension workers, and farmers.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints, agricultural experiences, and regional farming traditions.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code, agronomic models, and design.
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and smallholder farmers.

### Unacceptable Behaviors
Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical addresses or phone numbers, without explicit permission.
* Disseminating unverified or dangerous crop chemical/pesticide advice that violates government regulations or poses health risks to farmers.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests, and chat rooms), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project lead at **banquiladrian@gmail.com** or by opening a confidential discussion with repository maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
